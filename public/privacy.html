This app does not collect or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, logins, ads, or tracking.

Nothing. No names, emails, phone numbers, location, or contacts. No sign-up or login.

Designed for preschool children, the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, consistent with COPPA and applicable child-privacy laws.

Voice guidance plays audio bundled with the app, with the device's built-in text-to-speech as a fallback. The app never records you, and requests no microphone, camera, or location access.
